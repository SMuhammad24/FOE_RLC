Theoretical Foundation • Resistance

Ohm's Law & Internal Charge Dissipation

A resistor is a passive two-terminal electrical component that implements electrical resistance as a circuit element. It restricts charge carrier flow by inducing inelastic collisions between conduction electrons and lattice ions, converting kinetic energy into thermal dissipation.

Ohm's Law
EQUATION
V = I · R
Potential difference (V) is directly proportional to current (I) across resistance (R).
Resistivity Formula
GEOMETRY
R = ρ (L / A)
Where ρ is material resistivity, L is helical track length, and A is cross-sectional area.
Joule Heating / Power Dissipation
P = I² · R = V² / R
1 Watt = 1 Joule/sec
Helical Grooving
SPEC
Precision laser cutting forms a spiral resistive carbon track. Pitch controls overall resistance value.
Ceramic Core
SPEC
High alumina ceramic rod provides supreme thermal conductivity and structural rigidity under heavy load.

Electromagnetic Principles • Inductance

Faraday's Induction & Lenz's Opposition Law

An inductor resists changes in electric current. According to Faraday's law of induction and Ampère's circuital law, current generates magnetic flux inside the ferromagnetic core. Any change in current induces an opposing back-EMF.

Faraday-Lenz Law
EQUATION
v(t) = L · (di / dt)
Induced electromotive force opposes the instantaneous rate of current change (Lenz's Law).
Stored Energy
B-FIELD
E = ½ · L · I²
Energy stored reversibly in the magnetic flux volume within core permeability.
Inductive Reactance (AC Opposition)
X_L = 2π · f · L = ωL
Current lags voltage by 90° (ELI)
Ferromagnetic Core
FLUX
Boosts relative permeability (μᵣ) thousands of times over air, concentrating magnetic flux lines.
Lamination & Ferrite
EDDY
Insulated lamination stacks suppress parasitic eddy current losses and high-frequency overheating.

Electrostatic Storage • Capacitance

Dielectric Permittivity & Charge Polarization

A capacitor stores electrical energy in an electrostatic field between conductive plates separated by a dielectric medium. When a voltage is applied, equal and opposite charges polarize on the foil surfaces, resisting sudden voltage changes.

Capacitance Formula
EQUATION
C = (ε · A) / d
Where ε = ε₀·εᵣ is dielectric permittivity, A is plate area, and d is nanometer thickness.
Stored Energy
E-FIELD
E = ½ · C · V²
Energy stored across the dielectric dipole alignment under electric potential V.
Capacitive Reactance (AC Inversion)
X_C = 1 / (2π · f · C) = 1 / ωC
Current leads voltage by 90° (ICE)
Anodized Al₂O₃
NANO
Ultrathin molecular dielectric barrier layer (< 100 nm) yielding extreme capacitance density.
Electrolyte Contact
IONIC
Conductive liquid/gel electrolyte maintains atomic contact into microscopic etched foil crevices.
